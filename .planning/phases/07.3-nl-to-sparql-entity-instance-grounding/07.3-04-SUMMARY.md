## Decisions Made

- Prefixed `label_predicates` (`rdfs:label`, `pv:name`) are expanded to full IRIs via a small hardcoded prefix map (`_PREFIXES`) inside `grounding_index_builder.py` rather than parsing a `PREFIX` preamble out of the ontology Turtle -- CK25's prefixes are fixed and this keeps the builder self-contained and dependency-free of ontology-text parsing.
- `GroundedEntity.id` is stored bare (IRI brackets stripped in the builder), matching the existing `SparqlAdapter`/spike convention; the recall test re-wraps retrieved ids with `<...>` only at comparison time against the `_IRI_RE`-extracted gold IRIs.
