## Decisions Made

- NL-ACC-01 closes via the **significant-lift path**: McNemar p=0.0039 (well under 0.05), b=9/c=0 (zero regressions), bootstrap CI excludes 0. This is the plan-sanctioned pass condition (mirrors the 07.3-06-PLAN.md acceptance criteria and the NL-FEW-02/07-04 precedent structure, but with the significant outcome rather than a null).
- The fresh zero arm's pass_rate (5/49=0.1020) differs from the already-committed `openai-gpt4o-mini-ck25` entry (6/49=0.1224, captured in 07.2-04, a prior session). This is expected — gpt-4o-mini is not bit-deterministic even at low temperature (README Pitfall 6) and a different session can resolve a different dated snapshot. The confirmatory comparison deliberately uses the fresh same-session zero arm, never the stale committed number, so this discrepancy does not affect the confirmatory result's validity.
- Per-case `judge_note` D-05 bucket counts (candidate_engine_rejected / gold_engine_limitation) were not captured in this sweep's paste-back — only `name`/`passed`/`elapsed_ms` per case were recorded in the reports. This is documented as an explicit limitation in the baseline.json `note` field rather than silently omitted or fabricated.
