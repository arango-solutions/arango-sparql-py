**1. [Documented finding] One CK25 example landed at K=2 paraphrases, not K>=3**
- **Found during:** post-checkpoint verification of the committed bank (`min(len(paraphrases) for e in examples) == 2`)
- **Finding:** "How many BillOfMaterial are there for V104-2082346 (64)?" has only 2 paraphrases; the novelty-forcing budget (k*5 attempts at temperature 0.7, per Deviation Fix #1) was exhausted without producing a 3rd genuinely distinct phrasing for this short count-query template.

Non-blocking: `verify_generated_bank.py` (which gates REQ-1/REQ-2, not paraphrase count) still exits 0, and `FewShotIndex` retrieval never reads the `paraphrases` field.
- **Files affected:** None (data fact about the already-committed bank, not a code change)
- **Impact on plan:** None on the ADOPT decision.

**2. [Documented finding] The SPEC §11.2 shape-coverage metric is underspecified between two readings**
- **Found during:** computing shape-coverage of held-out failing cases against the bank's shape repertoire
- **Finding:** A literal canonical-skeleton equality reading (the exact `_skeleton()` abstraction the overlap audit uses) yields a degenerate 0%, because the generated bank's skeletons are near-disjoint from the corpus's gold skeletons BY DESIGN -- that disjointness IS the overlap-exclusion axis (D-05), not a coverage gap. A coarse shape-TYPE membership reading yields 100%.
- **Resolution:** Recorded the coarse reading (100%, clears the >=70% bar) together with the caveat explaining the literal reading's degeneracy, in both `baseline.json` and `REQUIREMENTS.md`, rather than silently picking whichever reading clears the bar or reporting the degenerate 0% without context.
- **Files affected:** `tests/nl2sparql/eval/baseline.json`, `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` (documentation only)
- **Impact on plan:** None on the ADOPT decision; the caveat is preserved for future readers of this evidence.
