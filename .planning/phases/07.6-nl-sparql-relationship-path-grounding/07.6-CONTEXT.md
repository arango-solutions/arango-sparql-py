# Phase 07.6: NL→SPARQL relationship-path grounding - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

An engine **seam-8** (`ClassPathIndex`) that, given a question's grounded **anchor
class** (from seam-6 entity grounding) and its **target** (from seam-7's token
scorer), retrieves the *specific* shortest predicate path connecting them from a
mechanically-built, **subclass-aware, inverse-edged** TBox class-connectivity
graph, and injects it as a compact navigation hint composed *after* the entity
block. Purpose: convert the 16 verified *right-entity, wrong-path* CK25 failures
(valid SPARQL that executes to empty) into correct navigations, with zero
regressions — surfacing *one path between two anchored classes*, NOT a schema dump
(the distraction that sank 07.4's full predicate grounding). Mechanical / TBox-only
→ transfers to CDF; Cypher inherits.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

These are HOW decisions from this discussion. They sit on top of the locked design
decisions **D-1…D-10** in `.planning/research/relationship-path-grounding-scope.md`
(depth ≤3, inverse edges required, D-9 subclass-aware nodes, D-10 bounded
self-revisit, ≤5-path budget, deterministic ordering, honest no-op fallback) — do
not re-open those.

### Build sequencing
- **D-01:** **Build `ClassPathIndex` directly in the engine** (`arango_query_core`
  local checkout at `~/dev/arango-query-core`), not as a throwaway eval prototype.
  The Step-0 offline recall spike (GREEN, 12/16 → ~16/16) already validated the
  *hypothesis*, so 07.5's "eval-prototype-first to validate the hypothesis" stage is
  redundant here. The committed offline recall gate (R4) runs against the **real
  engine seam**, and the engine is **published to both remotes + pin-bumped only
  after path-recall ≥14/16 holds against that real seam** (07.5-06 precedent).
  — **Reversibility:** costly — undo means reverting a published engine commit on two
  remotes and a `pyproject.toml`/`uv.lock` pin bump; sequence recall-gate-green
  *before* the pin bump so a gap surfaces while the change is still local.

### Anchor/target resolution
- **D-02:** **Distinct types × top targets, capped by the ≤5-path budget.** Feed
  every *distinct* `rdf:type` among the seam-6 grounded matches as an anchor class
  (usually 1–2 in practice) and the top few seam-7 token-scorer targets as
  destinations. Do **not** restrict to strict top-1 anchor / top-1 target — that
  risks missing cases where the correct anchor isn't the top-ranked grounded entity
  (several of the 16 target cases). The ≤5-path budget (D-05 below) is the
  anti-distraction safety valve, not a hard top-1 cut. Reuses seam-6/7 unchanged
  (SPEC constraint).

### Path-surface budget
- **D-03:** **One global pool, top 5 overall.** Pool every candidate path across all
  (anchor, target) combinations, rank by the locked deterministic ordering
  (length → question-token overlap → lexical signature), and take the top 5. Not
  per-destination fair-share — global ranking is simpler, fully deterministic, and
  naturally favors the tightest, most question-relevant paths. This is the concrete
  realization of the D-5 ≤5-path anti-distraction knob across multiple pairs.

### Renderer presentation
- **D-04:** **Lock the shape, tune the wording by test.** Locked now: the
  shared-variable **join/star** form (`?p pv:memberOf <Dept> . ?p pv:hasManager ?m`),
  **not** a directed A→B→C walk (Step-0 note — ck25-7/35 are join patterns);
  compact; ≤5 paths; placed *after* the entity block. The exact phrasing / header
  text stays **provisional and tunable during the human-run credentialed sweep**
  (D-7 "test, don't freeze"; the 07.4 lesson that wording is itself a distraction
  lever). — **Reversibility:** reversible — renderer wording is local to
  `format_prompt_section`.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact `path_grounding:` config-block field naming/shape (mirror
  `grounding:`/`predicate_grounding:`).
- The class-connectivity graph's internal representation (node keying, edge/inverse
  storage) and the shortest-path enumeration algorithm — within depth ≤3, ≤5,
  subclass-aware (D-9), bounded self-revisit (D-10), deterministic ordering.
- The offline recall-gate fixture format and where the 16 gold nav-paths are
  transcribed from (the candidate dump). Constraint: the gold-path fixture is
  **test-only** and MUST NOT leak CK25 vocabulary into the engine seam (the engine
  stays mechanically TBox-derived — 07.5 no-ontology-branch precedent).
- The exact `format_prompt_section` wording (per D-04).

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Canonical target case (names the lever): ck25-7 "manager of the Data Services department" — gold is the **inverse** join `?person pv:memberOf <dept-41622> . ?person pv:hasManager ?result`, not the model's invented forward `<dept-41622> pv:responsibleFor …`. The renderer MUST present this as a shared-variable join (D-04), and the graph MUST link `Employee ⊑ Agent` (D-9) or it silently drops this exact case.
- The ≤5-path budget is *the* specific control for the 07.4 distraction regression — global-pool top-5 (D-03) is how it's enforced across multiple anchor/target pairs.
- Recall-gate fixture (gold nav-paths) is test-only; hand-transcribing golds there does NOT violate the engine's no-hand-curation constraint (the engine derives everything mechanically from the TBox).
