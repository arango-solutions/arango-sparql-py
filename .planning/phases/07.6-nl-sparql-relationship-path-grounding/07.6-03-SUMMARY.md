### The substantive finding: offline-recall-green vs live-composition-null

Plan 01's offline R4 path-recall pre-gate is **GREEN** (14/16 empty-result golds have their gold predicate path mechanically retrievable by `ClassPathIndex.shortest_paths`, matching `SPEC.md`'s floor exactly) and stayed green through the engine promotion (re-confirmed against the pinned, non-editable install). Despite this, the live end-to-end composition shows **no conversions** (b=0) and **one distraction regression** (c=1) when the path hint is added additively on top of an already-strong `g+f` composition (16/49, this session's best arm). Mechanical path-recoverability — can the engine *find* the right predicate path — did not translate into end-to-end accuracy — does the *model* use that hint correctly once grounded entities and few-shot examples are already present in the same prompt. This is the first sweep in this codebase to measure the second capability directly, and the two are now empirically shown to be separate.

This mirrors two prior documented-nulls under identical discipline:
- **NL-ACC-02** (07.4, seam-7 predicate grounding): mechanically-sound predicate index, but additive composition on entity-grounding alone was a non-significant wash (p=0.6875, 2 gains/4 losses).
- **NL-FEW-02** (07, dense few-shot): dense-vs-zero was non-significant (p=0.453).

`g+f` (grounding + generated few-shot, 16/49) is now confirmed as the strongest composition available after **4 grounding-lever phases** (07.3 entity / 07.4 predicate / 07.5 few-shot / 07.6 path) — neither seam-7 nor seam-8 improves on it additively; both close via documented-null.
